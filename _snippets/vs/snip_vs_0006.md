स्वस्वरूपं **निर्मलं + अपारम्** — 
निर्मलत्वमात्रोक्तौ साङ्ख्यमतसिद्धात्मन्यतिव्याप्तिः।
तस्य वस्तुपरिच्छेदेन अपारत्वाभावान्नातिव्याप्तिः।

<div class="translation-inline" markdown="1">
ātmā is nirmala (devoid of stain); ātmā is apāra (limitless). Other entities might be pure in themselves. But ātmā is not limited -- even by the possibility of a second entity apart from itself.
</div>
