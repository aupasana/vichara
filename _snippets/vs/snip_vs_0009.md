निसर्गत एव सर्वेषामन्तःकरणे 
मलविक्षेपावरणात्मकदोषत्रयम् आस्ते।

<div class="translation-inline" markdown="1">
Three doṣas are innately experienced by mankind -- mala (pāpa saṁskāras), vikṣepa (unsteadiness in śravaṇa) and āvaraṇa (acceptance of ignorance).
</div>
